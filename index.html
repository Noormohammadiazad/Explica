Paper & physics

Hold it like a book

Turn a page
Drag it from anywhere — slowly to peel, briskly to flick. Click a page or press ← → to turn automatically.
Direction
Switch to RTL for right-to-left books: page 1 opens on the left and pages turn to the right. PDFs that declare a direction open that way.
Catch a page
Grab it while it is in the air.
Copy text
Press and hold on a line until a word lights up, then drag to select. Copy it, or pick a colour to highlight it. ⌘/Ctrl+A selects the open pages.
Annotate
The tools on the left: Highlight (drag across text), Pen, Note (click to pin one, click it again to edit) and Eraser. Read (R) gives the pages back to turning. ⌘/Ctrl+Z undoes. Everything stays in this browser, tied to the file — the PDF itself is never changed.
Turn & tilt
Drag the background, or Alt-drag anywhere — the book follows your hand. Sideways turns it about its spine; down lifts it towards you.
Pan
Right-drag, Shift-drag, Space-drag, or two-finger scroll.
Zoom
Wheel, pinch, or + / −. It follows the cursor.
Reset view
Double-click the background, or press 0.
Touch
One finger turns pages or the book; two fingers pinch, pan and twist.
